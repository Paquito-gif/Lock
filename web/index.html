Burns that lower the total supply

Elysian Pyre calls the token’s own burn function, so the supply really goes down. A token without one has its tokens sent to the dead address.

Every burn is counted per token, and the contract never keeps a single burned token.

What does a lock or a burn cost?

0.1 HYPE per lock or burn, plus gas. The fee can never go above 1 HYPE: that cap is written into the contract.

Can anyone else withdraw my tokens?

No. Only the lock’s owner can withdraw, and only after the unlock date. The admin has no function that moves tokens.

What happens to a permanent lock?

The tokens stay in the contract for good. Nobody, the owner included, can ever withdraw them.

Is the Elysian Pyre contract audited?

Not yet. Its source is public with 49 tests, and anyone can check the deployed code against it.
