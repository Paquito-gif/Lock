PYRE

Lock tokens until a date or forever, or throw them in the fire. One open-source contract on Elysium.

Scroll
01 / Lock

One padlock, one date.
Nothing opens before.

Pick a token, an amount and a date. The tokens stay in the contract until that day. After it, only the lock’s owner can withdraw them.

02 / Seal

Some padlocks
have no key.

A permanent lock never opens, not even for you. It is the plainest way to prove that liquidity stays where it is.

03 / Nobody else

Even the admin
stays outside.

The admin sets the fee, capped at 1 HYPE in the code, and nothing else. No pause, and no function that touches your tokens.

04 / Burn

What goes into the fire
never comes out.

Elysian Pyre calls the token’s own burn function, so its supply really goes down. Without one, the tokens go to the dead address. Every burn is counted.

Three rituals

supply after the burn = supply before − amount burned
